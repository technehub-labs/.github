# Governance

Governance, lifecycle, contribution, standards and organizational assets: the mechanisms by which the portfolio itself is controlled.

OpenDEAM development follows an explicit change pipeline: Investigation, Finding, Recommendation, Decision, ADR or Change Request, Implementation, Validation, Release. Repository implementation does not itself establish normative architectural or semantic decisions; normative decisions land through numbered Change Requests in [`dea-metamodel/change-requests`](https://github.com/technehub-labs/dea-metamodel/tree/main/change-requests) and Architecture Decision Records in the relevant foundation repositories.
