# Concepts

Conceptual and exploratory models representing domains, patterns and emerging areas. Concept repositories compose references, tools and artifacts on top of the DEA catalogs; they are where new architectural territory is explored before it is formalized into the model.

The autonomous enterprise pattern family is the current focus: one umbrella repository plus four sibling layer repositories (flow, networks, operations, workflows).
